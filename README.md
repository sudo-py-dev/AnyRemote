# AnyRemote

<img src="docs/assets/anyremote-icon.svg" width="96" height="96" alt="AnyRemote app icon">

Turn your Android phone into a remote for your TV, air conditioner, fan, projector, and more.

**[Download the latest APK](https://github.com/sudo-py-dev/AnyRemote/releases/latest)**

## What you need

- **Android 8.0 or later**
- **A phone with a built-in infrared (IR) blaster** to send commands

## Get started

1. Open the latest release and download the APK from **Assets**. Open it on your phone and follow Android’s installation prompt.
2. In AnyRemote, tap **Add remote** and pick your device type and brand or model.
3. Point your phone at the device and tap the test button. If the device responds, save the remote. If more codes are available, try the next one.

## Make it yours

- Save and rename remotes so your favorites are easy to find.
- Pin a saved remote to your home screen, or open **Settings → Add to Quick Settings** for faster access to the app.
- Choose a light or dark theme and switch among 11 languages.
- Use remote controls offline. Builds with in-app updates check GitHub for new releases.

Compatibility depends on the exact model. If yours is missing, [open an issue](https://github.com/sudo-py-dev/AnyRemote/issues) with the brand and model number.
